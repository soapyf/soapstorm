# Atmo Magic: far clouds — 10km horizon reach, distance LOD, distant rain shafts

Status: **design** (synthesized 2026-09-05 from an extend-the-deck and a tiled-LOD competing design + adversarial review; the stateless extend-the-deck shape leads, the tile design's iteration-geometry insights grafted, its cache rejected). Depends on `atmo_magic_wind_profile.md` (drift/frames) and `atmo_magic_storm_dynamics.md` (per-cell precip intensity for shafts). Debug: `atmo_magic_debug_views.md` V3 (LOD tiers/budget) and V4 (virga) ship with the matching phases here.

## 1. The finding that reframes the task: no tiles needed

The brief asked for LOD *sim tiles* with neighbour stitching and region-crossing upscale/downscale. The review's conclusion: **that machinery is unnecessary here, and building it would be a regression.** The cloud field is already a pure function of `(air-frame position, seed, weather scalars, wall clock)` — nothing is per-region, so region crossings already change nothing; "a storm seen far away is the same storm up close" already falls out of determinism; and detail can increase toward the camera as a *continuous function of distance* inside the existing single per-frame rebuild. The tiled design's persistent cache was rejected on a fatal: cached puffs bake camera-distance alpha and sun-dependent form/buried at *rebuild-time* camera/sun state — visible output would depend on camera history and diverge between clients standing at the same spot. The only cache that earns its keep is the ground-shadow bake's existing quantized key.

What survives from the tile design as requirements on the storm layer: one canonical position-keyed storm evaluator that every consumer calls (no per-tier approximations), with quantized outputs wherever a cache key consumes them.

Also corrected by review: today's *drawn* reach is not actually 10km. The base veil hard-fades to zero at 9.8km starting from 6.8km (`ssVolCloudF.glsl:455`), the far band already emits ~1 puff/cell past the squash knee, and the 2520-puff budget's erase-from-front makes the coverage-dependent true puff edge ≈**6.9km at high coverage** — a moving ring, not a horizon. "Render out to ~10km" is therefore mostly an *edge-quality* fix, not a reach extension.

## 2. The plan

Ordered; each step ships alone.

1. **Circularize the cell walk.** `buildDeck` walks a 79×79 *square* of cells; corner cells out to 14.3km are gated, noise-sampled, and spawn puffs only to be distance-rejected per-puff — ~21% pure waste. Reject cells by centre distance before the gate. Zero visual change.
2. **Radial LOD tiers** (revised 2026-09-05, Q3: budget concentrates near the camera; the far field gets cheaper bodies, not deletion):
   - **Tier A — fine** (0 to ~5km): today's 260m cells, `puffs_per_cell` ramping 3→2→1 with distance (crossfade the alpha of the highest sub index across each band edge; sub 0 always placed). **Every gate-occupied cell stays populated** in this tier — the stride-2 decimation idea was rejected here because it empties 3/4 of occupied cells while `ss_cell_occupied` and the shadow bake still report them occupied (desync: shadows and veil carve painting cloud where none draws), and a 2× stand-in migrates cloud mass up to 260m sideways in a camera-locked ring (the "swimming" failure the tessellation code documents as already-fixed once).
   - **Tier B — merged macro-puffs** (~5km to `FIELD_DRAW_M`): one large puff per occupied **macro-cell** (520m or 1040m, an air-frame coarsening of the same lattice), gated by a macro-scale evaluation of the *same* seed/cluster field so tiers agree about where cloud exists, radius/alpha scaled to conserve aggregate coverage, crossfaded against Tier A over a blend band. This is the review's rejected decimation made acceptable by distance: at ≥5km the residual disagreement between a 260m veil carve and a macro body is below what the eye can resolve through the haze/squash, whereas at the 1.6km squash knee (where the old draft put it) it was a visible desync. The macro gate is one more replication to keep in lockstep wherever the veil carve overlaps the tier — document the boundary radius as a shared constant beside `CELL_M`.
   - **Tier C — impostors** (beyond `FIELD_DRAW_M`, stretch to ~20km): no volumetric bodies at all — camera-facing cluster cards (a handful per distant storm/deck blob, sprite-shaded from the deck texture with the same gloom/sun terms) and/or the dome-band density bias (§2 step 6), living entirely beyond the veil. Super-distant storms on the horizon read as silhouettes + rain curtains, which is all that survives 15km of atmosphere anyway.
3. **Fix the edge — fade, don't cut.** Replace the budget's hard erase with a deterministic distance-weighted thin: keep far puffs by `hashUnit(cx,cy,sub) < keep_frac(dist)` and scale surviving alpha to conserve aggregate coverage, so the outer edge is a stable fade instead of a coverage-dependent moving cliff. Lift the veil's hardcoded 6800/9800 fade into uniforms derived from `FIELD_FADE_START_M`/`FIELD_DRAW_M` so deck + veil genuinely reach `SS_DECK_EDGE_M` (9800) where the dome band's horizon melt takes over. Follow-ups this forces: rescale the shadow bake's optical depth against the thinning (or distant ground stays over-shadowed by puffs that no longer draw), and add hysteresis to the `under_on_top` deck-order comparison (both decks' mean distances move differently as far populations change — order can flicker).
4. **De-tile the presence field.** `SS_NOISE_TILE_M = 2048` means the tower geography repeats ~5× across a 10km view. Puffs hide it (triplanar+skew) and the sheet hides it (Penrose 5-way read), but the presence carve and `noiseFieldAt` do not — at horizon reach it becomes a visible pattern. Add a second incommensurate octave to the presence read, **matched in all three replications** (builder, fragment, bake). This touches the near sky too; expect a gate-constant retune.
5. **Distant rain shafts** (§3).
6. **Dome horizon-storm bias** (stretch): distant storms beyond the deck edge darken/thicken the cirrus band at their bearing — a 2D scalar read at dome-UV resolution, not geometry. Needs the storm layer to expose bearing+distance; explicitly deferred.

Two dominant per-frame costs sit *outside* this plan and are noted as separate perf items: the veil's ~4900-quad immediate-mode emission every frame, and the ground-shadow bake re-running 6400 gates + 65k texels on every camera cell crossing.

### Phase 6a outcome (2026-09-05)

Steps 1–3 landed as `ssdecklodcore.h` + builder wiring, camera-only by contract (39 unit tests, a two-camera scenario, and `out/lod_top_view.png` showing the new smooth-thinning field beside the old 2520-body cliff at ~6.9km). Review corrections folded in: the circular walk is padded by the maximum puff displacement (`SHEAR_CAP_M + HERO_SHIFT_CAP_M + jitter`) because puffs are drawn up to 2.9km from their cell centre, and the per-puff 10km cull is gone — `edgeFade` (zero at 9.8km) owns the edge, which also restores "sub-puff 0 is always placed for every gated cell". The veil's rails moving from 6800/9800 to 8000/9800 is an intended value change: puffs and veil now share one rail pair.

### Phase 6b outcome (2026-09-05)

Step 4 landed as `ssdecknoisecore.h`: a second read of the same noise map at 1/φ scale and 37° rotation, mixed 0.65/0.35, applied at every presence and `n_map` read on both sides (seven CPU sites through the one `noiseFieldAt`, three GLSL sites through `ss_noise_mapDetiled`) — the opus review enumerated every read and found none left on the single read. `out/detile_top_view_presence.png` shows the ~5× horizon repeat on the left and none on the right. **Calibration lesson:** the first retune preserved the *tail* shares (share of cells fully cut / fully towered) and would have deleted 18–50% of occupied cells, because the builder consumes the continuous smoothstep, not the tails. The correct retune scales the old windows about the map mean by √(variance ratio) = 0.738 — hole 0.16/0.52 → 0.264/0.530, tower 0.42/0.78 → 0.456/0.722 — which preserves occupancy within ~5% at coverage 0.3/0.5/0.7; the relationship lives in the core (`retunedEdge`) and the pinned test asserts occupancy, not tails. Honest residual: the rotated second lattice cannot be aligned by the drift wrap, so the second octave re-samples at a wrap on top of the already-accepted hash re-roll.

### Phase 8g outcome — the veil law (2026-09-06)

The third build's top-down shot showed **no veil at all**, and the cause was in the sheet's density line rather than in any of the tuning: `base * presence * occupancy * edgeFade * near_fade`, where `occupancy` is the *puffs'* per-cell coverage gate and `edgeFade` is the *puffs'* outward-only rail. The veil was a strict subset of the puff footprint with no fade-in term anywhere, so it was thinnest exactly where the puffs were sparsest and gone entirely past 9800 m.

The law now lives in `ssveilcore.h` (`SSVeil`), transliterated by the sheet branch and driven by the sheet draw:

- **`holes` kept, LOD dropped.** The coverage gate's bilinearly softened holes stay — a hole in the deck is a hole in the veil. The far thinning and the edge fade do not: they are camera-distance render decisions, and `ssdecklodcore.h`'s own contract says LOD never speaks about the field.
- **`rimIn` — the fade-in.** `rimIn(d) = 1 - keepNorm(keepFrac(d)) * edgeFade(d)`, the exact complement of the share of rim presence the puffs still draw, so `rimIn + puffRimShare == 1` identically. 0 inside `THIN_START_M` (5000), 1 at `DECK_EDGE_M` (9800).
- **`rimIn` weights the deficit, not the base.** The obvious `base * rimIn` product is wrong in both directions — starting at 0 it deletes the deck-floor veil, starting at 1 it cannot rise — so the amplitude is `base + (RIM_FULL - base) * rimIn`, with `RIM_FULL` 0.90 above the near-field cap of 0.75 (that cap's reason, the black-slab-overhead read, is a near-field reason and does not apply at grazing angles through 8–14 km of haze). Consequence: **inside `THIN_START_M` the new law is bit-identical to the old one**, and it is `>=` the old law at every distance, strictly greater across the rim.
- **`reachOut` — the reach.** The veil keeps going past the puffs on rails of its own, 10000 → 14000 m (`VEIL_REACH_M`), on 520 m blocks beyond the subdivided 260 m field. This is a **placeholder for §8c**: the horizon deck's annulus replaces it when built.
- **The tiling is one partition, not two rings.** The draw walks 520 m blocks once, culls a block on its own centre against 14 km, and subdivides the blocks whose centre falls inside 10 km into 260 m children on the puffs' own lattice — so every surviving block is tiled exactly once. Costs: 4640 + 1124 = 5764 sheet quads against 4644 before (+24%); subdividing every surviving block would be 9136. Gap-free and overlap-free by construction *and* measured — the first cut ran two centre-culled loops on two tile sizes, which left 260 m radial notches at 9.9–10.3 km beside double-blended strips (668 and 464 of the 29 225 sampled points on a 120-ray × 10 m sweep of 9–11.5 km, camera at (0,0)), exactly where the veil sits at `RIM_FULL`; `unit_veil_sheet_partition.cpp` sweeps both and keeps the two-ring emission as its failing control. `mEffRadius` rises to `max(FIELD_RADIUS_M, REACH_END_M)` so the outer blocks are not folded onto one drawn radius by the squash.

Ladder: `unit_veil_law.cpp` (11 rungs, incl. the failing control `SSVeil::veilAlphaOld`), `unit_veil_sheet_partition.cpp` (4 rungs, the partition's coverage against the two-ring control), `twin_veil_law.cpp` (6 rungs, GLSL↔core over 375 100 samples, worst |err| 1.19e-07).

### Phase 6d outcome (2026-09-05)

Tier B landed as designed: occupancy is a tally of the fine gate's own verdicts (no fourth gate), coverage conservation is an exact identity for 2×2 blocks, and the two tiers crossfade over a 600 m band. The review caught the crossfade reading two different distances — fine cells the block's horizontal drift-only centre distance, the body its own 3-D hero-shifted altitude-leaned one — so at deck altitude both tiers drew at full weight along the band's inner edge. Both halves now read one scalar carried from the fine loop. The same pass moved the deck's structural shading (veil, fine puff, macro body) into a single core, pinned bit-identical to the old inline blocks by a transliteration twin. Beyond 10 km (impostors, §4) remains unstaged pending a frame-time measurement on a build.

## 3. Distant rain shafts (virga curtains)

Rain falling from distant cells rendered as fog-like curtains — cloud-consistent shading, no collision concerns.

- **Driver**: `precip_intensity × presence` at the cell, from the same in-loop data the builder already computes (`precipNoiseAt`'s inputs). Ships first against the global precip scalar (whole-sky virga under a storm — already coherent); switches to the storm layer's per-cell intensity when that lands. Threshold `SS_VIRGA_THRESHOLD`, scaled inversely by a new Weather Influence row ("Distant rain").
- **Geometry**: per qualifying cell, a vertical stack of ≤`CELL_M`-tall **Z-axis-billboarded** cards from `precipBaseZ()` down to ground (track floor / low-res terrain average; where no ground answer exists, fade the bottom out rather than terminate on a guessed plane). Card-stacking matters twice: per-vertex squash stays accurate (the veil's own comments document how large quads reconstruct world position tens of metres off-plane under the radial squash), and a world-fixed quad would vanish edge-on where a Z-billboard never does. Taper narrower toward the ground so it reads as virga, not a rain box.
- **Sorting**: shafts are emitted **into `mPuffs` itself** so the existing farthest-first painter's sort interleaves them correctly with puffs (a separate pass cannot — a 3km shaft behind a 2km puff would blend in the wrong order). Flagging: the spare `b` vertex channel is free again (phase 4 read meso gloom from the storm sample instead), so shafts flag via **`b` = 1** — no sign trick needed.
- **Shading**: re-run the puff `shade`/`form` formulas at the shaft's column with buried pinned ≈ `SHEET_BURIED`, so a shaft under a dark storm base inherits the gloom and a lit edge stays lit — same lighting, not a second model. Vertical alpha falls off toward the ground; distance fade shares the deck's own edge ramp. **Real alpha, sky forward pass only** — never post-deferred additive (constraint 4; a grey curtain must not bloom).
- **Handoff to particle rain**: the precip sim already owns a camera-tiered radius (`TIER_SHEETS`, default ~224m, with `tierBands()` crossfades). Shafts skip cells inside `r2 × 1.1` and ramp alpha up over a few hundred metres from `r2` — one shared radius, two systems fading through it in opposite directions, so no gap and no double-density band as weather changes.
- **Budget**: `SS_MAX_SHAFTS` (~64), trimmed by a **stable hash threshold** (`keepHash`: keep iff `hash(cell) < cap / quantised(n)`), never by distance and never by list index — the phase-6c review showed an index-based skip re-picks the whole kept set every time the candidate count moves (camera walk crossings, precipitation easing), popping curtains kilometres apart every few seconds.
- **Card stack** (phase-6c review): consecutive cards **overlap** by the shader's soft margin so the two-sided soft ends crossfade — abutting cards punched a transparent band at every seam. The slab layout lives in the core (`cardSpan`), not the emitter.
- **Far ground lift** (phase-6c review; retuned phase 8e, F8 2026-09-06 review): beyond the squash knee the curtain's bottom lifts off the ground, starting at 1.5× the knee and reaching 30% of the span by 3× the knee — physically virga (rain evaporating before it lands), and it keeps ground-level cards out of the far-squash depth fold, where drawn depth (~1.7km for a 5km curtain) would otherwise paint them over terrain standing in front. The later start and lower ceiling than the original phase-6c numbers (was: starting at the knee, ~55% of the span by twice the knee) leave near and mid curtains reaching the water/ground reference instead of lifting early. Stated residual: mid-air cards against tall terrain still meet the fold, as far puffs do.
- **Budget backstop**: the hash trim targets a *share*, so a rare excursion can keep more than the budget; a hard ceiling of 1.3× the budget (`hardCap`) then cuts by rank on a fixed per-cell key. That is an order statistic, not a reshuffle — growing or shrinking the kept set by one moves at most one curtain across the cut. It is the one stated exception to "a cell's keep decision depends only on the cell, the weather and the quantised count".
- **Outcome (2026-09-05)**: phase 6c closed after the hardening pass; the harness measured the quantum-crossing flip share at 0.2% against 0.3% expected (an index trim reshuffled 100%), and the seam union alpha at 0.75 against a 0.70 bound (abutting cards: 0.00). Clients whose precipitation LOD dial zeroes the sheets radius see no shafts at all — a per-client on/off by design of the shared-radius handoff.

## 4. Beyond 10km

Squash needs no mechanical change for any of this — it is a rendering fold on true distance, indifferent to how the distance was populated. The **20km stretch** (Q3) works like this: volumetric content (Tiers A+B) still ends at `FIELD_DRAW_M`; Tier C impostor cards populate ~10–20km, drawn through the same squash fold; the *simulation* (storm lattice, weathergen) already has no radius, so a 20km storm exists for free — only its representation is new. If Tier C proves worth more than cards, raising `FIELD_DRAW_M`/`SS_DECK_EDGE_M` in lockstep (and re-deriving the veil rails) extends the volumetric field instead — a constants change, not an architecture change — but the cell walk area grows quadratically (79×79 → ~154×154 gates at 20km), so that route requires the macro-gate to be the thing walked out there, never the fine 260m grid. Cards first; measure; then decide.
